Transparent model evaluation

Models are selected using 4-fold cross-validation on the training partition, then evaluated on a held-out 20% test partition. Scores on synthetic data measure the artificial task, not clinical performance. CSV-trained models also remain unvalidated.

Your data & this prototype

Account hashes, sessions and confirmed assessments are stored in the application's local SQLite database. Original uploads and chat messages are not retained by the application. File parsing may use operating-system temporary storage; filesystem encryption and temporary-file handling are deployment responsibilities.

Health inputs are not application-encrypted at rest. Use fictional data locally. Before real patient use, arrange clinical validation, consent, encrypted storage, access review, retention, backups and legal/compliance review. Account deletion removes active records but not external copies or backups.
